\subsection{Scheduling Policies in the Wild}
\label{sec:background-scheduling}

Scheduling policies have been studied extensively, with different designs targeting objectives such as fairness, throughput, and tail latency.
Beyond these objectives, they also differ in \textit{when} scheduling decisions occur during execution.
Following the classification in the SOAP framework~\cite{scully2018soap}, we group common scheduling policies into three classes:

\emph{Non-preemptive policy} is only activated by the end of a task.

\emph{Fully preemptive policy} allows a task to be interrupted at any time.

\emph{Threshold-based preemptive policy} allows a task to be interrupted at specific points.
